Abstract

This conference paper considers the cost and lifespan of bridge deck repairs.
Different repair options can be compared by considering their costs and
expected lifespans.

Introduction

Bridge deck repairs are important for maintaining the safety and durability of bridges. Selecting a repair method requires consideration of its cost and expected lifespan. Engineers must compare the financial cost of a repair with how long the repair is expected to last. This helps inform decisions about the maintenance and long-term performance of bridge infrastructure.
