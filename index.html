Abstract

This conference paper considers the cost and lifespan of bridge deck repairs.
Different repair options can be compared by considering their costs and
expected lifespans.

Introduction

Bridge deck repairs are important for maintaining the safety and durability
of bridges. Selecting a repair method requires consideration of its cost
and expected lifespan.
